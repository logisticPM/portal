Where the signal lives — agreement finds bugs, not conventions
Cross-family agreement is a high-precision filter for correctness defects — not a substitute for project-specific linting.
What it shows

As more independent model families agree on a finding, the ones that survive are increasingly universal functional bugs — wrong in any codebase — rather than project-specific rule violations.

functional-bug recall at 3 families agreeing
18%
rule-violation recall at the same depth
This is why recall plateaus near 0.83: much of the residue is lint-targetable convention, which agreement was never going to recover. Pair decorrelated review with a deterministic linter.
